04 / Jayagoo size guide & QC guide

Keep the useful columns.

A Jayagoo spreadsheet finds collection becomes more useful when each entry includes comparable evidence. Use these fields in your own notes or spreadsheet.

For clothing, measure a similar garment you already wear. For headwear, compare circumference and adjustability. For jewelry, check length, diameter and fastening dimensions.

FieldWhat to record
Item & variationExact product link, color, selected size and seller reference.
MeasurementsRelevant dimensions, units, measuring method and stated tolerance.
MaterialsSeller-stated composition; mark unverified claims clearly.
Photos & QCImage date when available, visible details and unanswered questions.
Price & currencyListed item price, currency and the date you checked it.
Additional costsApplicable domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and import charges.
Terms & statusCurrent availability, dispatch estimate and cancellation or return conditions.

Comparison rule: compare the estimated total payable in one currency. An unknown shipping charge is an unresolved cost, not a zero.

05 / Clear answers

Before you
build your list.

Practical answers about product discovery, sizing and the limits of photo-based comparisons.

What is a Jayagoo spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Jayagoo spreadsheet means a product comparison list used to organize fashion links, sizes, prices and notes. This page is an independent guide, not an official Jayagoo service or a downloadable spreadsheet.

How do I use this page to find products?

Choose a category near the top of this page to open its CAFANS listing. Review the destination product information, then save useful links and comparison notes in your own spreadsheet.

What should I check before choosing a size?

Compare the seller’s measurements with a similar item you already own. Check the measurement units, how each measurement was taken, and any stated tolerance. A familiar size label alone is not enough.

What can QC photos tell me?

Quality-control photos can help you inspect visible color, stitching, shape and some measurements. They do not independently establish authenticity, material composition, long-term durability or exact fit.

Are Jayagoo and JoyaGoo the same name?

This page uses the search phrase “Jayagoo spreadsheet.” It does not verify whether Jayagoo, JoyaGoo or similarly spelled services refer to the same business. Confirm the exact service name and official website before using an account or making a payment.

Does the guide guarantee brand availability or authenticity?

No. Brand imagery is included for editorial reference. It does not establish CAFANS inventory, authorization, brand affiliation or the authenticity of a particular listing. Verify the individual seller and supporting product evidence.
